Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Short version: we collect your email, age range and country so we can tell you when the Geet app launches. We don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it anytime.

Who we are

Geet ("we") is an iPhone app in development that helps groups of friends stay accountable. This policy covers the Geet waitlist website.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Your age range (for example, 16–17)
  • Your country
  • The date and time you signed up

We don't collect your name, location, contacts or payment details on this site. We don't use ad trackers.

How we use it

  • To email you when early access opens, and about your free first month
  • To understand where our users are, so we can set fair prices by country
  • To turn on the right safety settings for people under 18

We won't sell, rent or trade your information, and we won't use it for advertising.

Age requirement

Geet is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you choose "Under 13," nothing is saved. If you believe a child under 13 has signed up, contact us and we'll delete it.

Who else handles it

We use outside services to host this site and store signups. They process your information only to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist information until 12 months after the app launches, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe using the link in any email we send.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing privacy@YOURDOMAIN.com.

We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll email people on the waitlist.
